Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information ContentFlow Studio may process, why it is processed and how connected account permissions are handled.

Effective date: October 13, 2026

ContentFlow Studio is designed to support short-form content creation, media processing and authorized publishing workflows.

ContentFlow Studio does not request or store the passwords of users' connected social media accounts.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on which features are used, ContentFlow Studio may process:

  • Account identifiers returned by connected platforms.
  • Authorization and access tokens required for API operations.
  • Scripts, captions, topics and content metadata.
  • Images, audio, video and other media used in content production.
  • Publishing status and identifiers returned by connected platforms.
  • Technical workflow information required to process user requests.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Prepare and organize content ideas.
  • Generate or process scripts and captions.
  • Create and process images, narration and video assets.
  • Store or retrieve authorized media files.
  • Publish content through connected platform APIs.
  • Check and record publishing status.
  • Maintain security, reliability and workflow operation.

3. Connected Social Media Accounts

ContentFlow Studio may request authorization to connect to supported social media services.

Access is limited to scopes and permissions explicitly approved by the user through the third-party platform's authorization process.

ContentFlow Studio does not obtain access beyond the permissions granted by the account owner.

4. Authorization Tokens

Access or refresh tokens may be processed to securely communicate with authorized APIs.

Tokens are used only to perform authorized operations such as querying account information, uploading content or publishing content.

5. Content and Media Files

ContentFlow Studio may process scripts, images, audio and videos supplied by users or generated as part of a requested workflow.

Temporary media files may be stored locally during processing and removed after successful completion or transfer to designated storage.

6. Third-Party Services

ContentFlow Studio may communicate with third-party providers required for features such as:

  • Artificial intelligence processing.
  • Cloud file storage.
  • Media rendering and audio processing.
  • Social media authentication.
  • Authorized content publishing.

These providers maintain their own terms and privacy practices.

7. Sharing of Information

ContentFlow Studio does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when required to perform functionality requested or authorized by the user, or when required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, complete authorized workflows, maintain records or comply with applicable obligations.

Temporary production files may be automatically deleted after completed processing.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction of information.

However, no system connected to the internet can guarantee absolute security.

10. User Control and Revocation

Users may revoke authorization granted to a connected third-party platform using that platform's account, security or application settings.

Revocation may prevent ContentFlow Studio from continuing to perform related automated functions.

11. Data Deletion Requests

Where applicable, users may request deletion of information associated with their use of ContentFlow Studio.

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security or operational reasons.

12. Children's Privacy

ContentFlow Studio is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection would be prohibited by applicable law.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ContentFlow Studio, applicable law, service providers or third-party platform requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.

14. Contact

Privacy-related questions or requests may be submitted through the official ContentFlow Studio website or the contact method provided by the service operator.
