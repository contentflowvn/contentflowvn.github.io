Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ContentFlow Studio handles information when users access or connect to its content creation, automation, and publishing services.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, ContentFlow Studio may process information such as:

  • Account identifiers provided by connected third-party platforms.
  • Authorization tokens required to access connected services.
  • Video titles, captions, scripts, images, audio, and other media content.
  • Publishing status and identifiers returned by social media platforms.
  • Technical information required to operate automated workflows.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to provide requested functions, including:

  • Creating and processing short-form video content.
  • Generating scripts, images, voice, and other media assets.
  • Managing automated content workflows.
  • Uploading or publishing content to authorized third-party platforms.
  • Monitoring the status of publishing operations.
  • Improving reliability and functionality of the service.

3. Social Media Account Access

ContentFlow Studio may request permission to connect to supported social media accounts.

The service only accesses information and performs actions within the permissions explicitly authorized by the user.

ContentFlow Studio does not request or store users' social media account passwords.

4. Access Tokens

Authorization tokens may be used to communicate securely with third-party APIs.

Tokens are used only for authorized operations and should be protected using appropriate security controls.

5. Sharing of Information

ContentFlow Studio does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when necessary to provide requested functionality, such as cloud storage, artificial intelligence processing, media processing, or social media publishing.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain workflow records, comply with legal obligations, or support authorized publishing operations.

Temporary local media files may be deleted after successful processing and storage.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Choices

Users may revoke permissions granted to connected third-party platforms through the relevant platform's account or application settings.

Revoking permissions may prevent ContentFlow Studio from performing automated publishing or account-related functions.

9. Third-Party Services

Third-party platforms and services have their own privacy policies. ContentFlow Studio is not responsible for the privacy practices of those independent services.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the service changes or as legal and platform requirements evolve. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact ContentFlow Studio through its official website.
