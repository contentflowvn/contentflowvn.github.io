Features

One workflow for the complete content process.

ContentFlow Studio coordinates the major stages of short-form content production while helping creators maintain visibility and control.

01

Content Planning

Organize ideas, topics, scripts, captions and production status in one structured workflow.

02

Storytelling Scripts

Prepare structured scripts designed for educational, historical, informational and narrative-driven short videos.

03

Visual Production

Prepare image assets, carousel content and vertical visual media for short-form platforms.

04

Narration Workflow

Process narration scene by scene with pacing and timing suitable for the visual story.

05

Video Rendering

Combine images, narration and selected background audio into a finished vertical video.

06

Authorized Publishing

Use supported APIs and user-authorized accounts to manage publishing and publishing status.
